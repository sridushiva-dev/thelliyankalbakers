Corporate & diaspora

Your position

Strengths are real. The leaks are specific.

Keep

Since 1980

Three generations, year-long fruit, whiskey-style pack. Mambally’s sells heritage. You have heritage — the site doesn’t perform it.

Keep

1,000+ Amazon reviews

Proof the cake travels. Proof you don’t need to invent demand. You need to stop renting the relationship.

Fix

Trust at checkout

Generic theme plus courier mis-routes (including Delhivery) teach people the gift might not arrive like the unboxing.

Fix

Two prices, two stories

About ₹1,400 on your shop and ~₹1,100 on Amazon. Without a clear D2C reason — gifting, service, corporate — they stay on Amazon.

The right solution

Not a bigger catalogue. A Christmas system for one legend.

  • Not Shopify-plus-apps. You would still rent design limits, still fight shipping logic, still lack a pause switch you own.
  • Not a brochure website. Pretty pages without Razorpay, pincodes, and an order desk fail the week ads work.
  • Yes: one hero product. Grand Reserve first. Sizes and hampers later — Pandhal’s ladder, after the core converts.
  • Yes: Amazon stays. It is the showroom. The site is the counter where you keep the name, phone, and margin.
  • Yes: corporate as a lane. Form, lead list, GST-minded fulfilment — the KR Bakes lesson, inside your admin.

Admin you run
